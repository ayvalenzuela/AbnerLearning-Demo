Cycle view · the life cycle of a generative AI model, a loop that repeats with every new generation

Swipe sideways to see the whole picture.

  • A "token" is a small piece of text, often part of a word. Pre-training is just guessing the next one, repeated across an enormous dataset.
  • RLHF means reinforcement learning from human feedback: people rate or rank answers, and the model is trained toward the ones they prefer.
  • Red-teaming is deliberate attack testing, where people try to make the model produce harmful or wrong output before the public does.
  • Monitoring never really stops. Problems seen after launch become the data and tests for the next model.

Key takeaway: a GenAI model is never "finished". What users do after launch feeds straight back into the data that shapes the next generation.
