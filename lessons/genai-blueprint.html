Swipe sideways to see the whole picture.

  • The model writes one token at a time. Every new token goes back in as input, which is why replies stream out word by word.
  • The context window is the most text, counted in tokens, the model can take into account at once. Its size depends on the model.
  • Temperature near zero makes the sampler almost always pick the top-scoring token; higher values spread the choice across more candidates.
  • The model's learned weights do not change while it answers. It only knows newer or private facts if retrieval or a tool puts them in the prompt.
  • The token splits and scores shown are illustrative; real tokenizers split text in their own ways.

Key takeaway: a GenAI model doesn't look up an answer. It predicts the next token over and over, using attention over everything in its context window.
