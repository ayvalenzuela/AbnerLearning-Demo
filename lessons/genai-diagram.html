GenAI: inside a transformer block

Diagram view · the labelled anatomy of one transformer block, the part an LLM stacks over and over

Swipe sideways to see the whole picture.

  • Read the drawing from the bottom up: text goes in at the bottom and a guess for the next token comes out at the top.
  • Self-attention is how each token gathers context from the other tokens. In chat-style LLMs, a token can only look at the tokens before it.
  • The block keeps the same shape of numbers in and out, which is what lets it be stacked many times.
  • Many newer models put the "norm" step before attention and the feed-forward network instead of after. The parts are the same.
  • The word scores at the top are an illustration, not real model output.

Key takeaway: an LLM is mostly one simple block, attention plus a feed-forward network joined by shortcuts, stacked many times and used to guess the next token.
