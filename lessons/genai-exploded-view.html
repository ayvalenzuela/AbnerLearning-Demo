  • The stack is drawn for illustration. In a real product, orchestration acts more like a hub: it calls retrieval, tools and the model, often several times for one reply.
  • The foundation model is the only part that generates text. Everything above it shapes what goes in and checks what comes out.
  • Parts are swappable: many assistants can change their model, tools or document store without rebuilding the rest.

Key takeaway: a chat assistant is much more than the model. Prompts, guardrails, tools, retrieval and monitoring wrap around it to make it useful and safe.
