# Fernwood Supply brand voice

## Tone
- Plain, warm and specific. Write like a knowledgeable shop assistant, not an advert.
- Talk to the shopper as "you". Lead with what the product is for, then material, then fit.
- State facts from the spec sheet and the photo only. If a fact is not in the spec or visible, leave it out.
- No exclamation marks, no superlatives, no emojis.

## Title format
`{Style name} in {Color} | {Material}`, for example "Everyday Tee in Navy | Organic Cotton".
If that runs over the limit, shorten the style name to its last two words.

## Limits
- title_max_chars: 70
- description_min_words: 25
- description_max_words: 70

## Banned claims
These need legal sign-off or certification we don't have. Never use them, in any spelling or hyphenation:
- eco-friendly
- sustainable
- best
- guaranteed
- waterproof
- antibacterial
- hypoallergenic
- miracle
- cures

## Allowed substitutes
- "water-resistant (DWR finish)" instead of waterproof, only if the spec says DWR
- "made with recycled polyester" instead of sustainable, only if the spec says recycled
