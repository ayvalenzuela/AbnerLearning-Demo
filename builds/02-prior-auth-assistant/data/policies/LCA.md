# Lakeshore Care Alliance - Medical Policy Manual (fictional)

## Section 1.1 - Eligibility and member verification
Verify active coverage on the date of service. Requests for members with lapsed coverage are returned without review.

## Section 1.2 - Submission requirements
Each request must include the ordering clinician, CPT code, ICD-10 diagnosis, and chart notes supporting every criterion. Incomplete requests are denied and must be resubmitted.

## Section 1.3 - Turnaround and appeals
Standard requests are decided within 14 calendar days. Denials may be appealed within 60 days with additional documentation.

## Section 4.1 - Lumbar spine MRI without contrast (CPT 72148)
Lakeshore Care Alliance considers lumbar spine mri without contrast medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [LCA-72148-1] Low back pain persisting for at least 6 weeks.
- [LCA-72148-2] At least 6 weeks of conservative therapy (physical therapy or NSAIDs).
- [LCA-72148-3] Neurological deficit or radiculopathy documented on exam.

## Section 4.2 - Total knee arthroplasty (knee replacement) (CPT 27447)
Lakeshore Care Alliance considers total knee arthroplasty (knee replacement) medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [LCA-27447-1] Radiographic advanced osteoarthritis, Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4.
- [LCA-27447-2] At least 12 weeks of non-surgical treatment.
- [LCA-27447-3] Functional limitation in activities of daily living.
- [LCA-27447-4] Body mass index below 40.
- [LCA-27447-5] Documented tobacco-free status (non-smoker or quit at least 4 weeks prior).

## Section 4.3 - Attended polysomnography (in-lab sleep study) (CPT 95810)
Lakeshore Care Alliance considers attended polysomnography (in-lab sleep study) medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [LCA-95810-1] Epworth Sleepiness Scale score of 10 or higher.
- [LCA-95810-2] Witnessed apneas or loud habitual snoring.

## Section 4.4 - Laparoscopic gastric bypass (bariatric surgery) (CPT 43644)
Lakeshore Care Alliance considers laparoscopic gastric bypass (bariatric surgery) medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [LCA-43644-1] Body mass index of 40 or higher.
- [LCA-43644-2] Supervised weight management program for at least 6 months.
- [LCA-43644-3] Pre-operative psychological evaluation completed.
- [LCA-43644-4] Nutrition consult with a registered dietitian.
