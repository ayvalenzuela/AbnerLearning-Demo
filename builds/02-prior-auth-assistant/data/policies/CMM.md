# Cascade Mutual - Medical Policy Manual (fictional)

## Section 1.1 - Eligibility and member verification
Verify active coverage on the date of service. Requests for members with lapsed coverage are returned without review.

## Section 1.2 - Submission requirements
Each request must include the ordering clinician, CPT code, ICD-10 diagnosis, and chart notes supporting every criterion. Incomplete requests are denied and must be resubmitted.

## Section 1.3 - Turnaround and appeals
Standard requests are decided within 14 calendar days. Denials may be appealed within 60 days with additional documentation.

## Section 4.1 - Lumbar spine MRI without contrast (CPT 72148)
Cascade Mutual considers lumbar spine mri without contrast medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [CMM-72148-1] Low back pain persisting for at least 4 weeks.
- [CMM-72148-2] At least 4 weeks of supervised physical therapy.
- [CMM-72148-3] Neurological deficit or radiculopathy documented on exam.
- [CMM-72148-4] Plain radiographs (X-ray) of the lumbar spine completed.

## Section 4.2 - Total knee arthroplasty (knee replacement) (CPT 27447)
Cascade Mutual considers total knee arthroplasty (knee replacement) medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [CMM-27447-1] Radiographic advanced osteoarthritis, Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4.
- [CMM-27447-2] At least 12 weeks of non-surgical treatment.
- [CMM-27447-3] Body mass index below 40.

## Section 4.3 - Attended polysomnography (in-lab sleep study) (CPT 95810)
Cascade Mutual considers attended polysomnography (in-lab sleep study) medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [CMM-95810-1] Epworth Sleepiness Scale score of 12 or higher.
- [CMM-95810-2] Witnessed apneas or loud habitual snoring.
- [CMM-95810-3] Home sleep apnea test is non-diagnostic or clinically inappropriate.

## Section 4.4 - Laparoscopic gastric bypass (bariatric surgery) (CPT 43644)
Cascade Mutual considers laparoscopic gastric bypass (bariatric surgery) medically necessary when ALL of the following are documented:
- [CMM-43644-1] Body mass index of 40 or higher.
- [CMM-43644-2] Supervised weight management program for at least 6 months.
- [CMM-43644-3] Pre-operative psychological evaluation completed.
