## SOP-LOTO-01 - Lockout/tagout for product-contact parts
Stop the machine from the HMI. Isolate electrical and pneumatic energy, apply personal lock and tag. Bleed residual air. Verify zero energy by attempting a start. Remove locks only after a sanitation check.

## SOP-SAN-03 - Sanitation after opening product-contact parts
After any nozzle, valve or bowl work: CIP rinse, sanitize with approved chemical, swab test before restart. Record the swab result on the work order.
