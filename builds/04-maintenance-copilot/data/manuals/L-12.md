# Labeler L-12 (L-12) - Service manual (fictional)

## L-12 / E41 - Label skew
Alarm E41 on the L-12 means: label skew.
Likely causes, in the order the manufacturer recommends checking them:
1. [L12-E41-C1] Vacuum drum ports blocked. Steps: Clean drum ports with brush; check vacuum reads above 0.6 bar. Sensor sign: vacuum_bar below 0.6.
2. [L12-E41-C2] Bottle starwheel timing off. Steps: Re-time starwheel with timing pin; verify at slow jog.
3. [L12-E41-C3] Glue temperature low. Steps: Check glue roller temperature setpoint 165 C. Sensor sign: glue_temp_c below 150.
