# First call: Halvorsen Freight Group

**Deal:** Contact center modernisation (Discovery, $410,000)  
**Voxly:** Jamie Rivera, Enterprise Account Executive

## Attendees
- Dana Whitfield, VP Operations (economic buyer)
- Luis Moreno, Director, Customer Service (champion)
- Kira Jensen, IT Infrastructure Manager (technical evaluator)

## Objectives (45 minutes)
1. Confirm the top pain: callers hang up before they reach anyone.
2. Agree the ROI inputs (calls, abandon rate, hires, current costs).
3. Agree a pilot scope and who signs off.

## Discovery questions
- **Callers hang up before they reach anyone** - What happens to a caller who hangs up during peak today?  
  _Why we ask:_ "VP Ops said customers complain about hold times in peak season." (crm note)
- **New sites or a merger need one phone system** - How many sites must move, and in what order?  
  _Why we ask:_ "Integrating the depots of a newly acquired courier is the top IT project this year." (crm note)
- **New agents take too long to ramp** - How long until a new agent handles calls alone, and who coaches them?  
  _Why we ask:_ "New agents must ramp within two weeks; high turnover in the role." (job)
  _Thin evidence: one source only. Ask before we build on it._
