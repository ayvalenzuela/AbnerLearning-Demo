# First call: Quarry Lane Credit Union

**Deal:** Cloud phone migration (Discovery, $160,000)  
**Voxly:** Jamie Rivera, Enterprise Account Executive

## Attendees
- Greg Sandoval, CIO (economic buyer)
- Tess Albright, VP Member Experience (champion)

## Objectives (45 minutes)
1. Confirm the top pain: phone system is old and reaching end of support.
2. Agree the ROI inputs (calls, abandon rate, hires, current costs).
3. Agree a pilot scope and who signs off.

## Discovery questions
- **Phone system is old and reaching end of support** - When does support end, and what is the hardware refresh quote?  
  _Why we ask:_ "IT says the on-prem PBX reaches end of support in 2027." (crm note)
- **Call recording does not meet audit rules** - What did the last audit ask for that took the longest to produce?  
  _Why we ask:_ "PCI audit asks for call recording with pause-and-resume for card numbers." (crm note)
- **New sites or a merger need one phone system** - How many sites must move, and in what order?  
  _Why we ask:_ "Member services wants one phone system across branches after the merger." (crm note)

## Proposed next step
- Present migration plan to CIO, then a pilot plan within two weeks.
